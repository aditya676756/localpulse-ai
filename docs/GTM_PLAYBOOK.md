# 🚀 GTM Playbook: Getting Your First 5 Local Businesses in 7 Days

This playbook outlines the exact field-tested execution strategy to onboard 5 local businesses on a free 30-day trial and convert them into paying monthly software clients.

---

## 🎯 Target Business Profile (Ideal First 5 Clients)
Pick **appointment-driven, high ticket-frequency businesses** within a 3-mile radius of you:
1. **Hair Salons & Spas** (Average ticket: $40 - $120)
2. **Boutique Gyms & CrossFit Studios** (Average ticket: $60 - $150/mo)
3. **Specialty Cafes & Brunch Spots** (Looking for table reservations & catering pre-orders)
4. **Dental & Aesthetic Clinics** (Average ticket: $80 - $250)
5. **After-School Tuition & STEM Academies** (Average ticket: $100 - $250/mo)

---

## 📅 The 7-Day Sprint to 5 Signed Pilots

### **Day 1: Scout & Audit 15 Nearby Businesses**
- Walk down your local commercial market or browse Google Maps / Instagram.
- Check their WhatsApp / Contact link on their Instagram bio and Google Maps profile.
- Send a test message at 8:30 PM: *"Hey, do you have any haircut/booking slots tomorrow afternoon?"*
- Note which businesses fail to respond or take more than 4 hours to reply. **These are your prime targets.**

### **Day 2-3: The 3-Minute Walk-In Script**
Walk into the store during calm hours (e.g., 11:00 AM - 1:00 PM on Tuesday/Wednesday). Ask for the owner or manager.

> **Cold Walk-In Pitch Script:**
>
> *"Hi [Owner Name], my name is [Your Name], I live right down the street. I was looking at your Instagram and Google Maps page yesterday evening. Your work looks incredible, but I noticed there was no way for someone searching at night to instantly book an appointment without waiting until morning.*
>
> *I built an AI WhatsApp assistant specifically for [Salons/Gyms/Cafes] in our neighborhood. When someone messages your WhatsApp at 9 PM asking about prices or available slots, it answers instantly, takes their name, and books them right into your schedule.*
>
> *I'm setting up 5 local businesses this week for a **100% free 30-day trial**. No credit card, no setup fee, zero catch. If it doesn't bring you at least 25 new booked customers in 30 days, we disconnect it and you pay nothing.*
>
> *Can I set it up on your WhatsApp this afternoon? It takes literally 5 minutes."*

### **Day 4: Instagram DM / Direct WhatsApp Outreach (For Non-Walk-ins)**
If you can't visit in person, send a direct video screen recording or message:

> **Instagram DM Script:**
>
> *"Hey [Business Name] team! 👋 Quick question: when people message your Instagram or WhatsApp after closing time asking about prices or appointments, what happens to those inquiries?*
> 
> *Most local [salons/gyms] lose 10-15 customers a week to competitors simply because of slow response times.*
> 
> *We built a simple AI bot that connects to your WhatsApp and books appointments 24/7. We are offering a 30-day free trial to 5 local businesses in [City Name]—we guarantee at least 25 new bookings or you pay $0.*
> 
> *Want me to send a 60-second video of how it looks with your actual service menu?"*

## 📊 Step 3 Execution: The 30-Day "Proof of Results" Meeting

At Day 28 of the free trial, open LocalPulse AI and click **"Generate ROI Proof Report"**. Print it out or save as PDF.

Schedule a 10-minute sit-down with the business owner.

> **Conversion Script (Free Trial to Paying Client):**
> 
> *"Hey [Owner Name], congratulations on a great month! I pulled the numbers from the last 30 days of LocalPulse AI on your WhatsApp:*
>
> - *Total new appointments captured: **38 bookings**.*
> - *Estimated revenue added to your business: **$2,480**.*
> - *Dormant customers reactivated via WhatsApp follow-up: **14 clients**.*
>
> *You paid $0 for this trial. Moving forward, to keep the 24/7 AI concierge running and continue capturing after-hours bookings, our service is just **$79 a month** (less than $2.60 a day).*

**Expected Conversion Rate:** 70% - 85% of businesses convert when presented with concrete booking proof.

## 📈 Step 4 & 5: Monetization Tiers & Scaling to 20+ Stores

1. **Flat Subscription (Recommended for Simplicity):**
   - **Starter:** $79 / month (WhatsApp AI Bot + Google SEO Schema)

2. **Performance / Commission Model:**
   - $2.00 / completed booking (Tracked via WhatsApp ref code).
   - Ideal for skeptical owners who only want to pay on guaranteed outcome.

### **Unit Economics at 20 Stores:**
- 20 Stores × $99/month average = **$1,980 Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**.
- Infrastructure cost (Node.js VPS + Meta WhatsApp Cloud API): ~$40/month.
- **Gross Profit Margin: > 97%**.
